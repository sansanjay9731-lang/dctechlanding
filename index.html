When should I choose RoCEv2 over InfiniBand for a DGX cluster?
Aurilearn Good question — it comes down to cost, scale, and ops complexity. InfiniBand gives you lower latency and is the safer choice for large-scale all-to-all training workloads (>16 nodes). RoCEv2 makes sense when you already have an Ethernet fabric, want a single network for storage and compute, and can tune PFC/ECN correctly. For a DGX H100 pod under 8 nodes, RoCEv2 with a well-configured lossless Ethernet fabric is a legitimate choice. Module 4 covers the full decision matrix.
