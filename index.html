The curriculum

Eight modules, foundation to production

A deliberate path from how AI workloads behave to how you scale, secure and cost-optimise them in the real world.

01 AI Workload Lifecycle & Infrastructure Requirements
How training, fine-tuning and inference workloads behave — and the infrastructure characteristics each one demands.
02 GPU Computing Platforms & Architecture (H100, NVLink, sizing, TCO)
Inside modern GPU platforms: NVLink topologies, right-sizing for a workload, and total-cost-of-ownership trade-offs.
03 Bare-Metal & Kubernetes GPU Orchestration
Scheduling GPUs on bare metal and through Kubernetes — device plugins, node management and reliable orchestration.
04 High-Performance Networking Fabrics (RDMA, InfiniBand, RoCEv2)
The fabrics that make multi-GPU training viable: RDMA fundamentals, InfiniBand and RoCEv2 design choices.
05 GPU Resource Sharing & Multi-Tenant Management
Partitioning, quotas and fair-share scheduling so multiple teams safely share expensive GPU capacity.
06 Storage for AI Workloads (NVMe-oF, VAST, Weka, NetApp)
Feeding the GPUs: high-throughput storage architectures and how to keep data pipelines from starving compute.
07 Observability & Operational Excellence (DCGM, Grafana)
Instrumenting GPU fleets with DCGM and Grafana — the metrics, dashboards and alerts that keep clusters healthy.
08 Hybrid Scaling, Compliance & Cost Optimisation
Scaling across on-prem and cloud, meeting compliance needs, and driving down the cost of every GPU hour.
