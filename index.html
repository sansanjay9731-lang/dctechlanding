When should I choose RoCEv2 over InfiniBand for a DGX H100 cluster?
AuriLearn It depends on scale and existing infrastructure. InfiniBand is the safe choice for large-scale all-to-all training (>16 nodes). Lower latency, simpler to tune. RoCEv2 works well when you already have an Ethernet fabric and can correctly configure PFC/ECN. For a DGX H100 pod under 8 nodes, a well-tuned lossless Ethernet fabric is a legitimate and often cheaper choice. Full decision matrix in Module 4.
